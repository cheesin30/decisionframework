LTI LEARNING EXPERIENCE — PRESENTER SCRIPT
============================================================

Deck:     LTI_Learning_Experience_Capital_Learning.pptx (8 slides)
Audience: Capital Learning team
Runtime:  ~10-12 minutes + live demo + Q&A
Tone:     conversational, confident, business-impact lens.
          The slides are visual anchors — you carry the story.


------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 1 — COVER · "From a workshop to a journey"
------------------------------------------------------------
(~45 seconds. Let the slide sit while people settle. Speak plainly.)

Hi everyone — thanks for making the time.

I want to walk you through a piece of work that started with a simple
observation about our Long-Term Investing workshop: the workshop itself
is strong. Really strong. But it's a moment. Advisers walk in, they
have a great two hours, and they walk out.

What I've been building is everything AROUND that moment — so the
workshop stops being an event and becomes a journey. I'll show you what
I built, why it's shaped the way it is, and then I'll actually run the
whole thing live so you can see it work.

TRANSITION: "Let me start with what was missing."


------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 2 — THE GAP · "The workshop began and ended in the room."
------------------------------------------------------------
(~1 minute. Point to the empty boxes — let the visual do the arguing.)

This was the starting point. In the middle: the LTI workshop — good
content, well delivered, no complaints.

But look at either side. BEFORE the workshop: nothing. An adviser's
first contact with the material is the moment they sit down. AFTER the
workshop: also nothing. No reinforcement, no follow-up, no way to know
whether anything stuck.

And there was a third gap you can't see on the slide: no template.
Every consultant who runs this workshop starts from scratch on anything
beyond the deck itself.

So the learning lived and died inside one room, on one day.

TRANSITION: "That gap was also the opportunity."


------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 3 — PROJECT BRIEF · "Freedom to design it end-to-end."
------------------------------------------------------------
(~45 seconds. Sweep left to right along the chevron.)

Because nothing existed before or after the workshop, there was nothing
to inherit — which meant freedom to design the whole journey
end-to-end: pre-workshop, in-workshop, post-workshop, as ONE
EXPERIENCE, NOT THREE ASSETS.

I want to be clear this wasn't built in a vacuum — the team's guidance
shaped the direction throughout, and conversations with our consultants
in Singapore and the international team fed directly into how the
materials are framed.

The design principle was simple: everything connects. What advisers do
before the session sets up what happens in the room, and what happens
in the room feeds what they receive afterwards.

TRANSITION: "So here's what that actually looks like."


------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 4 — WHAT I BUILT · "Built the journey around the workshop."
------------------------------------------------------------
(~2 minutes. First substance slide — go card by card, left to right.)

Three layers, wrapped around the existing workshop.

FIRST — PRE-WORKSHOP. A short survey advisers complete right before
the session starts. Two minutes, tap-not-type. It gives them a reason
to start thinking about the material before we say a word — and it
gives us a read on the room.

SECOND — THE WORKSHOP MATERIALS. This is the core of it. Four pieces,
all designed to be printed and put in an adviser's hands after the
session:

  - the TIPSHEET — the whole workshop on one page
  - STEPS FOR SUCCESS — the follow-through sequence, so "what do I do
    Monday morning" has an answer
  - ADVISOR TALKING POINTS — the language for their own client
    conversations
  - and a CLIENT-FACING DECK — material they can use directly with
    clients, verbatim

The test for every one of these was: can an adviser use this in a real
client conversation without us in the room?

THIRD — POST-WORKSHOP. A follow-up survey, plus the piece I'm most
excited about: the ENGAGEMENT CALENDAR TOOL, which delivers materials
at timed touchpoints after the session. The learning doesn't stop when
the room empties — it keeps arriving.

TRANSITION: "Each of these exists for a specific reason — let me show
you the logic."


------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 5 — SOLUTIONS OVERVIEW · "Each material supports a specific moment."
------------------------------------------------------------
(~1.5 minutes. This is the 'it was thought through' slide.
Emphasise purpose, not features.)

Same materials, but now look at the WHY column for each moment.

The pre-workshop survey exists to UNDERSTAND THE STARTING POINT —
where each adviser actually is, not where we assume they are.

The advisor toolkit exists to HELP ADVISERS CONTINUE THE CONVERSATION
— the workshop gives them conviction; the toolkit gives them the words
and the structure to act on it with clients.

And the post-workshop layer exists to SUSTAIN ENGAGEMENT — because we
all know what happens to workshop material after two weeks of silence.

(Point to the navy band.) The engagement calendar is the connector. It
takes the toolkit materials and sends them out across regular
touchpoints — automatically. Nobody has to remember to follow up. The
follow-up is built in.

TRANSITION: "That's the theory. Now let me show you the real thing."


------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 6 — DEMO TRANSITION · "Now, the flow in action."  (dark slide)
------------------------------------------------------------
(~30 seconds, then switch to the live demo. The dark slide is your cue
that the mode is changing.)

Rather than describe it any further — let me just show you.

I'll walk through the experience exactly as an adviser would: from the
survey before the workshop, to the advisor materials, to the follow-up
calendar landing in your inbox.

LIVE DEMO — RUN SHEET:

  1. PREPARE — open the pre-workshop survey; complete it on screen
     (or have a volunteer do it on their phone).

  2. DELIVER — flick through the four toolkit pieces quickly; hold up
     printed copies if you have them.

  3. FOLLOW UP — open the engagement calendar tool, generate the
     follow-up, then ask the room:

     "Now grab your phones — scan this code. In about thirty seconds
     the LTI follow-up journey will land in your inbox. This is exactly
     what advisers experience at the end of the workshop."

  4. While calendars arrive, narrate what's happening behind the scenes
     in one sentence. When phones start buzzing, pause — let the moment
     land.

TRANSITION (back to deck): "So that's today. One more thing — where
this goes next."


------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 7 — FUTURE OUTLOOK · "Built for now. Ready for what's next."
------------------------------------------------------------
(~1 minute. Tone matters here: NOW is not a stopgap.)

What you've just seen is ready to use NOW — a practical consultant
toolkit. Nothing waiting on a platform, nothing waiting on a build. You
could run this at your next workshop tomorrow.

But it was deliberately built with the future in mind. The structure
you saw — survey, toolkit, timed follow-ups — is exactly the structure
that migrates onto CI PRO: hosted in one place, advisers sign up
themselves, all the materials live there.

So the work does double duty. It serves advisers today, and it's the
working blueprint that makes the CI Pro move straightforward when we're
ready — we'd be moving a proven structure, not designing from scratch
on a platform.

TRANSITION: "That's the journey — from a workshop that ended in the
room, to an experience that follows advisers out of it."


------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 8 — Q&A · "Questions?"
------------------------------------------------------------
(Close warmly, then open the floor.)

The ask from me is simple: try it with your next LTI workshop.
Everything is ready — the survey, the toolkit, the calendar.

Thank you — and I'd love your questions.

LIKELY QUESTIONS — PREPARED ANSWERS:

Q: How much setup does a consultant need to do?
A: Almost none — the materials are ready to print, and the calendar
   journey is generated in under a minute.

Q: Can this work for other workshops / markets?
A: Yes — the structure is workshop-agnostic, and the materials have
   already been adapted with input from the Singapore and international
   teams.

Q: What happens to the survey responses?
A: They're for the consultant's read on the room — light-touch by
   design, not a data-capture exercise.

Q: When could CI Pro happen?
A: The structure is migration-ready now; timing is a platform
   conversation, not a rebuild.

Q: Who maintains the materials?
A: Content designers own generation; consultants just use them — that
   split is designed in.
