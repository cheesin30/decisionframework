# Long-Term Investing — Live Poll Pack (Slido / Mentimeter)

The pre-workshop diagnostic, rebuilt as a **live audience poll** so it feels like
a warm-up game, not a form. Same diagnostic signal (objections heard, first move,
sticking point, confidence), collected in the room in ~2 minutes.

Everything below is copy-paste ready. Poll **types** are chosen to be the most
*engaging* format for each question — with a faithful fallback where a fancier
type isn't available on your plan.

**Global settings (turn on for the whole session):**
- Anonymous responses ✅
- Show results live as people vote ✅
- One device per person (or projector + show of hands)

---

## Poll 0 — Word Cloud opener *(icebreaker — 20 sec)*

- **Type:** Word Cloud · entries per person: **2**
- **Question:** `In one word — how does it feel when a client won't commit to investing?`
- **Why:** No wrong answer, everyone contributes instantly, the screen fills up
  and the room laughs. Perfect ice-breaker before the "real" questions.

---

## Poll 1 — What do you actually hear? *(replaces Survey Q1)*

- **Type:** Multiple Choice — **multiple answers ON**
- **Question:** `Which of these have your reluctant clients ACTUALLY said? (pick all that apply)`
- **Options:**
  1. `"Cash feels safer — I don't really need to invest."`
  2. `"The markets are too risky right now."`
  3. `"I'll wait until the timing's better."`
  4. `"Honestly… investing feels too complicated."`
- **Settings:** allow multiple selections; show live bars.
- **Facilitator line:** "The tallest bar is the objection we'll spend the most time on."

---

## Poll 2 — Your first move *(replaces Survey Q2)*

- **Engaging type:** **Ranking** — surfaces people's real instinct order
- **Question:** `A client just pushed back. Rank what you ACTUALLY reach for — first to last.`
- **Items:**
  - `Pull up market data / performance charts`
  - `Walk them through the product or fund features`
  - `Tackle the concern head-on with an explanation`
  - `Ask what's really driving the concern first`
- **Faithful fallback (if no Ranking on your plan):** Multiple Choice, single answer —
  question: `Gut instinct — what do you do FIRST?` with the same 4 options.
- **Reveal line:** "Most rooms rank *Ask what's really driving it* last — today's four-box
  framework flips it to **first**."

---

## Poll 3 — Where does it stall? *(replaces Survey Q3)*

- **Type:** Multiple Choice — single answer
- **Question:** `Be honest — where does the conversation usually STALL for you?`
- **Options** *(each maps to a box in the four-box framework)*:
  1. `Reading what's really behind the client's concern`  → **Acknowledge**
  2. `Explaining why long-term investing actually wins`   → **Perspective**
  3. `Answering "too risky / bad timing / cash is safer"` → **Perspective / Confidence**
  4. `Turning the talk into an actual next step`          → **Opportunity**
- **Facilitator line:** "Wherever this bar clusters is the box we drill hardest today."

---

## Poll 4 — Confidence check *(replaces Survey Q4 self-rating scale)*

- **Mentimeter type:** **Scales** (put all 3 statements on one slide)
- **Slido type:** **Survey** with 3 **Rating** rows (Slido Rating is one row per poll,
  so use a Survey, or run 3 quick Rating polls)
- **Scale:** `1 = Not confident` → `4 = Very confident` (label the ends; a 1–5 star scale is fine too)
- **Prompt:** `How confident do you feel TODAY? Slide each one.`
- **Statements:**
  1. `Explaining why sitting in cash can be its own long-term risk`
  2. `Responding in the moment when clients raise risk or timing concerns`
  3. `Moving from a client's concern to one clear, practical next step`
- **Tip:** Keep this poll and **re-run the exact same slide at the end** of the
  workshop — the confidence jump is your closing money-shot.

---

## Poll 5 — Payoff / bridge *(optional close of the warm-up)*

- **Type:** Multiple Choice or a single Word Cloud
- **Question:** `In one word: what do you most want to walk out of today able to do?`
- Then transition: "You just built today's agenda in two minutes. Here's the
  **four-box framework** — Acknowledge · Perspective · Confidence · Opportunity —
  that turns every one of these into a repeatable move."

## Fast build routes

**Slido — bulk import:** In your Slido event → *Content* → *Import content* →
download their spreadsheet template, paste the rows from `slido_import.csv`
(in this folder) into it, and upload. Ranking/Survey rows may need a quick manual
touch-up after import.

**Mentimeter — quickest path:** New presentation → add slides in this order:
Word Cloud → Multiple Choice (enable *Multiple answers*) → Ranking → Multiple Choice
→ Scales → Word Cloud. Paste the text above into each. Share the `menti.com` voting code.

> `workshop_slides/index.html` in this repo is a **self-hosted, offline version** of
> the same flow (tap-to-vote bars, confidence "temperature", four-box payoff) — handy
> as a backup if the venue Wi-Fi is flaky or you'd rather not use a code.
