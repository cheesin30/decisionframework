The bigger picture

Time in the market beats timing it.

Flip the chart above through crash after crash and the shape barely changes. Here's the whole twenty-year record — and the price of stepping out for just the best few months.

6 of those 10 best months landed within six months of one of the 10 worst. The rebound comes exactly when it feels most hopeless — so selling in the panic is how you miss it. Timing isn't wrong because it never works; it's wrong because the biggest days arrive without warning, right when the urge to sell is strongest.
