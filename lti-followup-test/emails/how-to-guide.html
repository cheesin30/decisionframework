How to add your LTI follow-up calendar

A 2-minute guide to opening the calendar file (.ics) attached to your email — on your phone or on your desktop.

What you received: a single calendar file called something like Firm_LTI_Follow_Up_2026-06-30.ics. Opening it adds three short follow-up touchpoints — with talking points already written into each event — to your calendar. You only need to open it once.

On your phone

iPhone (iOS)

  1. Open the email in Mail or Outlook.
  2. Tap the .ics attachment.
  3. Tap Add All (or Add) when the events preview appears.
  4. Choose which calendar to add them to if asked, then tap Done.

Android

  1. Open the email in Gmail or Outlook.
  2. Tap the .ics attachment to download/open it.
  3. Tap Add / Save when Google Calendar shows the events.
  4. If it doesn't open automatically, open Files → tap the downloaded .ics.

On your desktop

Outlook (Windows / Mac)

  1. Open the email and double-click the .ics attachment.
  2. The events open in Outlook's calendar preview.
  3. Click Save & Close (or Add to Calendar) for each, or Save All.

Google Calendar (browser)

  1. Download the .ics from the email to your computer.
  2. In Google Calendar, click the gear → Settings.
  3. Choose Import & export → Import, select the file, click Import.
Reminders are built in. Each touchpoint already includes a 15-minute reminder, so your calendar will nudge you at the right time — no setup needed.

Troubleshooting

  • Can't see the attachment on mobile? Open the same email on a desktop and add it there — it syncs back to your phone.
  • Tapping the file just shows text? Use Open in… and choose your calendar app (or Outlook).
  • Events landed in the wrong calendar? Delete them and re-open the .ics, choosing the calendar you want.
  • Still stuck? Reply to the email that sent you this file and the Capital Learning team will help.
